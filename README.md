## Website Goals and Objectives
### The main goal of my website is to create a professional, user-friendly website that can provide viewers with clear information about the business and its products or services. The website should be easy for the user to find information, contact the person who designed the business, and to take action by making an enquiry or purchasing the owner of the business product or service. 
### What I need for this website is to provide useful information for users to know about the small business, its products or services, prices, and contact information.  
### I need to make the website look professional but also be creative with websites so that it can catch the attention of the users so that they can recommend it to other people they know around them. 
### I need to increase the website traffic so that the users can have an easy way to find the website through search engines and share them on social media. I also need to improve customer experience by providing simple navigation and information that can be easy to understand. I also need to increase the conversion so that I can encourage customers to contact the person who owns the business, request information, purchase products, or use the service offered.
